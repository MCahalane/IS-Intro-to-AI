# Introduction to AI for Business

A standalone Information Systems learning module: what to delegate to AI, where people must stay involved, who is accountable, and how to keep your own understanding real when AI makes everything feel easy. Part of the [Information Systems Plug & Play Learning Modules](https://mcahalane.github.io/IS-Learning-Modules/).

## Contents (about 3½–4 hours)

1. **Know it**: human in the loop, why it matters, why the human isn't automatically better, autonomy / oversight / accountability.
2. **Explain it**: human in, on, over and out of the loop; worked examples; where humans enter the AI lifecycle.
3. **Apply it**: classify unfamiliar arrangements.
4. **Analyse it**: what decides AI autonomy; three fictional scenarios (Horizon Bank, SwiftMart, TalentMatch); Australian industry practice (Atlassian, Macquarie, CBA); the rubber-stamp trap.
5. **Judge it**: the Jagged Frontier field experiment (Dell'Acqua et al., 2026); centaur and cyborg workflows; reflection questions.
6. **Design it**: a design-the-loop template with a worked example and text download.
7. **Know what you know**: the illusion of explanatory depth (zipper activity), intellectual humility, five habits.
8. **Using AI tools well**: chat vs agent modes, tokens, matching tools to tasks, legal basics for inputs and outputs.

Plus a searchable glossary (38 terms), sources, and 28 ungraded quick checks with explanations.

## Files

- `index.html`: the module content.
- `assets/module.css`: UNSW Information Systems theme, shared look with the other modules.
- `assets/module.js`: quick checks, saved notes, zipper activity, design download, glossary search, navigation.
- `assets/*.jpg`: AI-generated teaching illustrations.

Answers and notes are stored only in the learner's browser (`localStorage` key `is-intro-ai-v1`). No tracking, analytics or accounts.

## Origin

Adapted from course seminar material (two seminar decks, a case-study reading, an Australian industry reading and a legal fact sheet). Course labels, assessment and administrative content, dated news items and institution-specific instructions were removed so the module stands alone.
